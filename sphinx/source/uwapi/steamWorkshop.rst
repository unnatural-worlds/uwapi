Steam Workshop
==============
The Workshop is the easiest way to obtain user generated content from other players.

Go to `Steam Workshop <https://steamcommunity.com/app/2369780/workshop/>`_, log in, and just subscribe to the content you like.
It will be automatically downloaded.

Maps and replays will appear in the respective folders.
Mods need to be explicitly activated in the respective menu in game.

.. note::
	You may need to restart the game an additional time to use the content after it has been downloaded.

.. note::
	When browsing maps or replays, there is button to go up a folder, just like files and folders on your computer.
