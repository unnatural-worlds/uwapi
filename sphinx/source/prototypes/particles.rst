Particles
---------
Particles behavior is hard-coded in the game.
Different types of effects are determined by a single id.

Overview
========

+-----------------+-------------------+
| ID range        | Category          |
+=================+===================+
| 1000 – 1999     | Weapon attack     |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 2000 – 2999     | Weapon impact     |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 3000 – 3999     | Weapon splash     |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 7000 – 7999     | Explosion center  |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 8000 – 8999     | Explosion impact  |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 11000 – 11999   | Unit hit          |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 12000 – 12999   | Unit death        |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 50000 – 59999   | Recipe processing |
+-----------------+-------------------+
| 100000 - inf    | Extra effects     |
+-----------------+-------------------+
